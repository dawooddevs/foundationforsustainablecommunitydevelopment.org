Who We Are

A Foundation Designed to Strengthen What Already Exists

The Foundation for Sustainable Community Development exists to help communities build the knowledge, systems, partnerships, and practical resources needed for long-term self-reliance.

Our role is not to replace local leadership. It is to strengthen it. Meaningful development is more durable when communities help define priorities, participate in implementation, and retain the skills and institutional capacity created through each initiative.

FSCD aligns community leaders, health care providers, educators, institutions, technology partners and funders with long-term community success.

Sustainable development is not simply about delivering assistance. It is about helping communities build the capacity to shape their own futures.
Foundation Framework

Vision Mission Purpose Recognized Public Charity

The Foundation’s direction is grounded in the mission, purpose, governance and planned activities reviewed as part of its federal tax-exempt approval.

01
Our Vision

Self-Reliant Communities

Self-reliant communities with inclusive and improved health care, education, economic opportunity for women, and local institutions enabling long-term sustainable development.

02
Our Mission

Community-Driven Development With Practical Support

The Foundation for Sustainable Community Development (the “Foundation” or “FSCD”) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to facilitating community-driven development in rural and underserved regions, including health care deserts, by expanding economic opportunity; improving access to health, education, and basic services; strengthening local institutions and community capacity; and promoting job training, workforce development, and economic participation for women and marginalized populations.

FSCD provides domestic and international leadership, technical assistance, internet connectivity, training, and additional resources upon verification of a request for proposal (RFP). FSCD plans to collaborate through strategic partnerships with medical equipment providers, hospitals, and community medical clinics that believe in our mission and purpose.

03
Our Purpose

Charitable Educational Scientific

This Foundation is organized exclusively for charitable, religious, educational, and scientific purposes, including, for such purposes, the making of distributions to organizations that qualify as exempt organizations described under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or corresponding section of any future federal tax code.

Leadership & Governance

Accountability Built Into the Foundation

FSCD is governed by a Board of Directors with expertise in sustainable development, public policy, nonprofit governance, and community engagement. The majority of the Board consists of independent directors with no financial interest in the Foundation.

The Foundation maintains contemporaneous records of Board meetings, resolutions, and grant approvals to support fiduciary oversight and compliance.
