Why Vision Matters

Vision Care Is About More Than Eyesight

Vision affects learning, work, mobility, independence, and quality of life. FSCD’s initiative is designed to make portable image capture and connected diagnostic review more practical in communities where specialist access may be limited.

Learning

Clearer vision can affect whether a student can follow classroom instruction and participate confidently.

Work & Independence

Vision can influence employability, mobility, daily function, and economic participation.

Earlier Attention

Imaging can help move concerning findings toward professional evaluation sooner.

Access

Portable image capture can reduce the distance between a patient and the beginning of a care pathway.

International Purpose • Initial India Deployment

Designed for Real Community Access

Training, Diagnostic Partnerships, and Accessibility enable the Eyer2 to function within a Vision Care Initiative built around verified local need, trained local teams, qualified health care partners, and practical community access.
