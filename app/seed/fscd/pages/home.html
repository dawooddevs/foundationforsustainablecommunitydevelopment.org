Vision Care Initiative

Earlier Detection Wider Access Local Skills

The planned initiative combines FDA-cleared ophthalmic imaging technology, portable retinal and ocular-surface imaging, digital connectivity, professional review, training, and community-based access

  • Eyer2 high-definition retinal and ocular-surface imaging
  • Software-assisted prioritization workflows and telemedicine connectivity
  • Training for medical assistants, nurses, and eye technicians
  • Free and low-cost examinations through qualified diagnostic partners
